# Credits

## Pixel art

**Tiny Dungeon** by Kenney is used for bundled pixel-art tiles and sprites.

- Official source: [Kenney — Tiny Dungeon](https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-dungeon)
- OpenGameArt mirror: [Tiny Dungeon](https://opengameart.org/content/tiny-dungeon)
- Bundled in this project at `assets/vendor/tiny-dungeon/`
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

CC0 permits copying, modification, and commercial use without attribution. Attribution is included here in appreciation of Kenney's work. The license file distributed with the asset pack is preserved at `assets/vendor/tiny-dungeon/License.txt`.

**Roguelike/RPG Pack** by Kenney is bundled as an additional CC0 environment and sprite library.

- Source: [OpenGameArt — Roguelike/RPG Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/roguelikerpg-pack-1700-tiles)
- Bundled at `assets/vendor/roguelike-rpg/`
- License file: `assets/vendor/roguelike-rpg/License.txt`

## Audio

The music and sound effects are generated procedurally at runtime with the Web Audio API. Their compositions, synthesis settings, and game integration are original to the Abyssal Weight project; no third-party audio files are bundled.
